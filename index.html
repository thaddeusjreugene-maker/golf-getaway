What the week looks like
ArrivalDrive down, check in, groceries, burgers at the house, pool.
Golf daysGolf in the morning, back by early afternoon. Non-golfers get the beach, St. Augustine, or the pool.
WednesdayGroup trip to historic St. Augustine for dinner and dessert.
ThursdaySteak night at the house.
FridayKing & Bear finale, then the final group dinner and golf awards.
SaturdayBreakfast, clean up, head home.
What's not included

Prices cover your share of the house and green fees with tax. Food, gas, and spending money are extra. Plan on about $35–$50 per person per night for groceries and one dinner out.
